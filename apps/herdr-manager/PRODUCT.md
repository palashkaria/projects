# Herdr activity layout experiment

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
A developer following attached agents across one Herdr server's workspaces. Answer: which layout best exposes what agents are doing and what needs attention?

## Prototype assumptions
The developer checks this beside their editor at a daylight desk, so use a white surface, readable compact text, and restrained burnt-orange selection accents. These are experiment choices, not validated brand decisions. The coordinator requested autonomous work, so no design interview is required for this throwaway branch.

## Principles
Preserve uncertainty. Raw status and inferred turn end are different facts. A pane title is context, not an authoritative task description. No completion/success claim. Include shells, empty workspaces, freshness, disconnected inventory. Compare three structures, not three color schemes.

## Accessibility
Keyboard controls, visible focus, textual status labels, wrapping long names, native form controls, reduced-motion support. No fake controls or mutations.
